VISIVRA Ingredients

1

Lutein:

Lutein is a naturally occurring carotenoid found in several green leafy vegetables, including spinach, kale, and broccoli. It is also present in the human eye, particularly in the macula, which is responsible for detailed central vision. Lutein is valued for its antioxidant properties and its role in supporting macular pigment. It helps filter certain wavelengths of light and contributes to protecting eye tissues against oxidative stress. Because the body cannot produce lutein on its own, it must come from food or supplementation. Its inclusion in VISIVRA makes it an important part of the formula for adults interested in maintaining healthy macular nutrition and supporting normal visual wellness.
2

Zeaxanthin:

Zeaxanthin is another carotenoid naturally present in the retina, especially in the central region of the macula. It works alongside Lutein as part of the macular pigment that helps protect the eyes from oxidative stress. This nutrient is commonly found in foods such as corn, orange peppers, and leafy green vegetables. Zeaxanthin is particularly interesting because of its connection with maintaining healthy macular function and supporting normal visual performance. In VISIVRA, it is combined with Lutein to provide complementary nutritional support. Including both carotenoids in a daily eye health formula offers a convenient option for people who want to pay more attention to their long-term eye nutrition.
3

Bilberry Extract:

Bilberry Extract comes from the small, dark berries of the bilberry plant. These berries contain natural compounds called anthocyanins, which are responsible for much of their deep blue-purple color. Anthocyanins are known for their antioxidant properties and are commonly studied for their potential health benefits. Bilberry has traditionally been associated with eye wellness, which is why it appears in several vision-support supplements. In VISIVRA, Bilberry Extract adds a plant-based antioxidant component to the formula. It complements the carotenoids and essential nutrients included in the product. Although it is a popular ingredient in eye supplements, strong evidence for treating vision problems with bilberry remains limited.
4

Astaxanthin:

Astaxanthin is a naturally occurring carotenoid pigment found in certain marine organisms, including microalgae. It is responsible for the reddish-pink color seen in foods such as salmon and shrimp. Astaxanthin is widely researched for its antioxidant properties and its potential role in supporting the body's response to oxidative stress. In eye health formulas, it is often included as a complementary antioxidant ingredient. VISIVRA combines Astaxanthin with Lutein, Zeaxanthin, and Bilberry Extract to provide a broader selection of antioxidant nutrients. Its presence makes the formula appealing to people interested in nutritional support for eye wellness. However, its inclusion does not guarantee relief from eye fatigue or improved eyesight.
5

Zinc:

Zinc is an essential mineral that the body needs for several important functions, including immune health, normal cellular activity, and metabolism. It also plays an important role in maintaining normal vision because it helps support the body's use and transportation of Vitamin A. Zinc is naturally found in foods such as pumpkin seeds, beans, nuts, meat, and seafood. Adequate zinc intake is important for maintaining healthy body functions, including those related to the eyes. VISIVRA includes Zinc as part of its nutritional formula, complementing its carotenoids and antioxidant ingredients. People should avoid excessive zinc supplementation, as taking too much can cause unwanted effects and interfere with copper absorption.
6

Vitamin B6:

The nutrient vitamin B6, known as pyridoxine, plays an essential role in protein metabolism and formation of neurotransmitters, as well as other physiological functions. Overdose of vitamin B6 leads to nervous disorders.
7

Vitamin A:

Vitamin A is an essential nutrient that plays a significant role in maintaining normal vision. It helps the eyes produce the visual pigments required to respond to light, particularly in low-light conditions. Vitamin A also supports normal immune function and the maintenance of healthy body tissues. It can be obtained through foods such as carrots, sweet potatoes, spinach, eggs, and dairy products. VISIVRA includes Vitamin A to complement the other nutrients in its eye-support formula. Adequate Vitamin A intake is important, but more is not always better.
